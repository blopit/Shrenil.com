Word Bubbles Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Word Bubbles is a daily word puzzle for iPhone and iPad. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't collect anything that identifies you.

What stays on your device

Your progress, scores, streaks and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Leaderboard times

When Post Times to Leaderboards is on (it is by default), finishing a board sends the following to our server at shrenp.com, so the app can show the fastest times:

  • which board it was (the day and level),
  • a country and region worked out from your device's time zone, and
  • your finishing time and score.

Nothing else is sent: no name, account, device identifier or precise location. The server keeps only the fastest time and score for each board and region, with the time it was set. It doesn't store IP addresses, and the data is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

You can turn this off any time in the app under ⋯ → Post Times to Leaderboards.

Children

Word Bubbles is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
