Style guide

From the CentrOS iOS app

This page documents the design system every page of this site is built from: the CentrOS iOS app’s sky, clear Liquid Glass sections, iOS system colours and text styles, and its floating control layer. Every page loads two shared stylesheets with no build step: public/css/style.css, and public/css/sky.css (the sky’s gradient ground, shown before the first WebGL frame); the case-study pages and this page add a small page-local file from public/css/pages/. Every token value below is read live from public/css/style.css with getComputedStyle, and every specimen is the real markup.

Design intent

5

When a new component is in doubt, it has to serve one of these.

  1. Every page stands on the skyA slowly drifting cosmic aurora with its own hue per page, dark by default as the app was designed. Content sits in clear Liquid Glass inset-grouped sections that read exactly like the app’s cards.
  2. Content first, controls float above itContent → glass control layer → person, never page → boxes → buttons. A large title opens each page; the only chrome is an account capsule, a floating tab bar with Contact set apart, and system-glass sheets. The chrome recedes while you read and returns when you reach for it.
  3. Colour means one thing everywhereThrough the app’s Tone: blue for action, yellow for waiting, green for settled, orange for caution, red for stop. Chrome stays label-coloured. Type is the system font in iOS text styles, and serif is only for words a person wrote.
  4. Simple is not minimalEvery approved fact stays one tap away in native disclosure rows that work without JavaScript. Nothing looks pressable unless it does something; anything that cannot act is drawn as a chip.
  5. Mobile first, flawless everywhereBuilt at 390 px and checked from 320 to 430 px, in 844 × 390 landscape and on desktop, where the iPad readable column sits centred on the sky. Reduced motion, reduced transparency, increased contrast, forced colours, print and no-JS are all handled.

Geometry

4

Concentric: a rounded thing inside another echoes its curve, so the inner radius is the outer one minus the inset.

  • Card

    Radius , continuous in the app. A section is one card with hairlines, or cards apart with 10 px of sky between.

  • Sheet → group , inset : 42 − 16 = 26.

  • Tab bar → selected capsule , inset : 31 − 6 = 25.

  • Pill

    Hit box (dashed) → visible capsule 32 px, from a 6 px transparent border. Every target is at least 44 × 44.

Glass

Content is the surface; controls float above it. No content sits inside a second pane of glass: wells inside cards use --fill-4.

When glass steps aside

  • Reduced transparency: cards become the system’s own opaque card (--bg-grouped-2) with no rim; controls, the sheet and the scroll edge turn opaque. The sky still shows between cards.
  • Increased contrast: a solid visible rim on cards and controls, stronger secondary labels and separators.
  • Forced colours: the sky is hidden; cards, controls, the sheet and chips get CanvasText borders.
  • No backdrop-filter support: glass falls back to near-opaque fills.

Flow

.flow .flow__step .flow__arrow .flow__label .flow__detail

failure paths
archived

Contract closed and durable. Evidence, tool calls, gate decisions, and approver (if any) are retained for audit. Read-only from this point — the audit trail outlives the runtime.

  • All 11 states, defined8 on the main path · 3 failure paths
    draft
    Contract materialized but not yet routed. Risk tier and lane assigned by the router on the next tick.
    routed
    Contract has been classified and assigned a policy lane. Lane A proceeds to executor; lane B/C waits for owner confirmation first.
    executing
    Executor is calling tools, pulling RAG context, and writing checkpoints. Crash here resumes from the last checkpoint, not the beginning.
    eval-pending
    Executor finished; adversary is critiquing the evidence trail. Low-confidence outcomes route to gate-pending or held.
    gate-pending
    Required gates haven't all passed. Verifier holds the contract until evidence/adversary/verifier all return ✓ or the owner intervenes.
    approved
    Owner approved a lane B/C contract from the inbox. Executor proceeds to perform the action.
    executed
    Action complete. Final state written. Contract awaits archival.
    archived
    Contract closed and durable. Evidence, tool calls, gate decisions, and approver (if any) are retained for audit. Read-only from this point — the audit trail outlives the runtime.
    rolled-back
    A gate detected an issue post-execution. Rollback gate reversed the action and the contract closed with a failure summary in the audit trail.
    held
    Owner clicked Hold from the inbox. Contract paused until evidence is refreshed or owner re-decides.
    stop-ship
    Critical gate (e.g., adversary flagged a hard violation) fired. Contract is hard-blocked and only an admin override can release it.

The CentrOS contract lifecycle. Nodes ship disabled, so without JavaScript they are static chips and the archived definition is already on the page. With JavaScript, one node at a time is aria-pressed and the detail is announced politely.

Motion

Nothing moves unless you do something, except the sky.

What moves, and when

9

  • State changeschevrons, the segmented thumb, the switch knob--ease-smooth, the app’s .smooth spring, over
  • Tab bar minimise and restorewidth, height and padding--dur-min on --ease-smooth
  • Sheet on phonesup from below, back down--ease-sheet over
  • Sheet from 768 pxscale .97 → 1 plus a fade0.35 s and 0.25 s
  • The sky settles behind a sheetdims, inside sky.js0.8 s
  • Sky hue and appearancecross-fade to new colours--ease-sky over
  • Pressrows fill instantly, out over 0.25 s; pills and buttons dim to .65, back over 0.2 sglass controls grow to 1.06 and brighten over 0.15 s
  • Inline title and scroll edgeopacity--dur-fade
  • On loadnothing movesevery transition is off until load plus one frame

Never animated: no keyframes, load animations, count-ups, scroll-linked effects or parallax on content. Under reduced motion the sheet fades instead of sliding and the tab bar and chevrons change instantly.

The sky

The slowest thing on the screen.

  • Baked, then made to flowSix keyframes are drawn once, then a light pass cross-fades them forward and back on a 60 s loop and carries the picture along a slow current.
  • Light on the deviceDrawn at about half the screen’s pixels and about a dozen times a second at rest; never redrawn for a scroll.
  • Parallax, the only oneThe picture is 1.35 screens tall and slides at 0.15 of the scroll.
  • Holds stillUnder reduced motion it is one still frame; it pauses while the tab is hidden and its clock stops, so it never jumps.
  • FallbacksA CSS gradient of each page’s sky shows before the first frame, without JavaScript, without WebGL and while a lost context comes back. Background: Solid draws one plain colour and no canvas. Forced colours and print hide it.

Accessibility

Every preference the browser exposes is handled in the last block of style.css, after every component, so it always wins.

  • Focus. Every control shows a 3 px blue :focus-visible ring (Highlight under forced colours). “Skip to content” is the first Tab stop and moves focus to main; scroll padding keeps a focused element clear of the top bar and the tab bar.
  • Targets. Every link, button and summary outside running prose is at least 44 × 44 px; pills draw a 32 px capsule inside a 44 px hit box.
  • Contrast. Every text pair passes WCAG AA against the rendered sky, sampled at several sky moments. --label-2 and the --fg-* tone colours are raised above the app’s values to get there, and a soft halo sits behind text set straight on the sky.
  • Reduced motion. The sky is one still frame, the sheet fades, and the tab bar and chevrons change instantly.
  • Reduced transparency. Cards become opaque system cards with no rim; controls, the sheet and the scroll edge turn opaque.
  • Increased contrast. Solid rims, stronger labels and separators, and inline links underlined at 2 px.
  • Forced colours. The sky is hidden; cards, controls, the sheet and chips get system borders, the current tab is outlined in Highlight, and the top bar turns opaque.
  • Print. Every disclosure opens before printing and returns to how you left it afterwards. The chrome, the sky and the demo frame are hidden, and external links print their URL.
  • No JavaScript. The site is dark with the CSS sky, every fact is reachable through native <details>, the tab bar stays expanded, the Contact buttons jump to the page’s Contact section, and lifecycle nodes stay static chips.
  • Structure. One h1 per page, an h2 for every section, and no skipped levels. Landmarks: one main, one primary nav, one footer.
  • Privacy. No cookies and no tracking. Local storage holds only an appearance or background you choose, in this browser.
