## Workflow

1. Pick the 2–3 candidates per category most likely to give a useful quote.
2. Send the matching template (`peer.md` / `manager.md` / `customer.md`).
3. When a quote comes back, paste it onto `/references/index.html` (private
   page, noindex). Also drop two or three named cards onto the
   `#references` section of `/index.html` and `/centros/index.html`.
4. Update the **Status** column in this file. Decline ≠ failure — just routes
   you to the next person.

Target state: 5 named references across 2–3 categories before the next round
of high-priority applications goes out.
